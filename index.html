Our Love Story

The Story of Aileen & Ivan

Their love story began in the most unexpected place—a school, where playful teasing became the beginning of something beautiful.

  1. ✎

    Playful Colleagues

    At first, Aileen and Ivan were simply colleagues who enjoyed teasing each other. Things took a turn when a fellow teacher created a group chat, which eventually led to their first meal together outside school. Back then, they were still part of a group, slowly getting to know one another.

  2. ✉

    An Unexpected Message

    When Ivan finally reached out to Aileen through a personal message, their story began to unfold. Ivan started courting her, but Aileen was hesitant at first. With a five-year age gap between them—and Aileen being the older one—she wasn't entirely sure about the idea.

  3. ♥

    A Heart That Said Yes

    But as time passed, their conversations grew longer, their laughter became more frequent, and they discovered that love was not about age, but about understanding, connection, and choosing each other.

Their birthdays fall just a day apart. Born one day apart in the calendar, they found themselves sharing a love that would bring their lives together.

What began with teasing, group hangouts, and simple conversations slowly grew into a love worth choosing every day. And now, the two colleagues who once barely knew each other are ready to begin their greatest adventure yet.

From schoolmates in the story of life to partners for a lifetime, Aileen and Ivan are about to say “I do”—proving that sometimes, the most beautiful love stories begin with a little teasing, an unexpected message, and a heart that learns to say yes.
